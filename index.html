    Nothing reaches students without a teacher's check. The original English lecture always stays on screen; translation helps but does not replace it.

    Why it matters

    • Students with hearing impairments, working students and students who missed a class get the same lecture.
    • Students with lower English can check a term in Kazakh or Russian and keep reading the original.
    • Kazakhstan's Concept of Inclusive Policy 2025–2030 moves from the medical model to removing barriers. Universities are not yet part of that chain.
    • Universal Design for Learning: several ways to receive the same content.

    Ethics and limits

    • Recording only with the teacher's consent; students are told before class.
    • Recordings processed on university servers, not public chat services.
    • Speech recognition is weaker on Kazakh and accented English, so human review stays required.
    • Notes must not replace attendance: no ready answers, questions ask for explanation.
